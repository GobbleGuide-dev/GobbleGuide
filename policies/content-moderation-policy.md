# Gobble Guide – Content Moderation Policy

**Last updated:** 14/11/2025

Gobble Guide is a user-generated content (UGC) platform where users can create, upload, and share recipes. To maintain a safe, respectful, and useful experience for all, we enforce the following **Content Moderation Policy**.  
This policy explains how content is evaluated, moderated, reported, and removed.

---

## 1. Overview

Gobble Guide allows users to post:

- Recipes  
- Photos of food  
- Ingredients lists  
- Cooking instructions  
- Optional notes and meal planning content  

To protect the community, all content must follow our **Terms of Use**, **Community Guidelines**, and the rules defined in this policy.

We use a combination of:

- Automated content detection  
- Manual review  
- User reporting  
- Behavior monitoring  

to maintain a safe environment.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content

The following types of content **are not allowed** on Gobble Guide and may be removed without warning.

### 2.1 Illegal or Harmful Content

Content that:

- Promotes illegal activity  
- Encourages violence or physical harm  
- Shows or suggests dangerous food practices

Examples:

- Recipes encouraging consumption of raw meat or unsafe food handling without proper disclaimers  
- Instructions involving illegal substances  

---

### 2.2 Hate Speech, Harassment, or Abuse

Content that:

- Targets individuals or groups with insults or discrimination  
- Encourages harassment  
- Uses slurs or degrading language

Examples:

- Harassing comments on a recipe  
- Offensive captions or descriptions

---

### 2.3 Explicit or Adult Content

Gobble Guide is not an adult-content platform.

Prohibited:

- Nudity  
- Sexualized images  
- Suggestive or explicit descriptions

---

### 2.4 Violence or Graphic Content

Content may not include:

- Graphic violence  
- Images of injury  
- Depictions of cruelty  

---

### 2.5 Spam, Scams & Misleading Content

This includes:

- Repeated posting of promotional content  
- Attempting to sell goods or services  
- Misleading recipes, false claims, or deceptive nutritional information  
- Links to unsafe or malicious websites

---

### 2.6 Copyright-Infringing Content

Users must not post:

- Content copied from other creators without permission  
- Images they do not own  
- Recipes from copyrighted cookbooks, websites, or apps unless they are original or sufficiently adapted

---

### 2.7 Malware or Technical Abuse

Includes:

- Uploading malicious files  
- Attempting to disrupt normal use of the app  
- Using bots, scrapers, or automation tools

---

## 3. Restricted Content

Certain content is allowed but may require moderation or warnings.

### 3.1 Sensitive Diet or Health Information

Allowed with restrictions:

- Recipes promoting diets (keto, vegan, etc.)  
- Nutrition-related tips  

Not allowed:

- Medical claims (“This recipe cures diabetes”)  
- Unsafe practices (“Eat this to replace medication”)

We may add disclaimers or remove content.

---

### 3.2 Potential Food Safety Risks

Recipes that may involve:

- Raw eggs  
- Raw seafood  
- Uncooked ingredients  

may be flagged and reviewed.  
If necessary, a food safety notice will be displayed or the content may be removed.

---

## 4. Moderation Process

Content moderation happens through one or more of the following:

### 4.1 Automated Moderation Tools

The app may use automated systems to detect:

- Offensive language  
- Spam  
- Adult content  
- Copyright violations  
- Known unsafe patterns

Automated actions may include:

- Hiding content  
- Marking content for review  
- Blocking uploads

---

### 4.2 Manual Review

Our moderation team reviews:

- Reported content  
- Content flagged by automated tools  
- Accounts showing suspicious behavior  

Moderators may:

- Remove content  
- Issue warnings  
- Restrict account features  
- Suspend or ban accounts

---

### 4.3 User Reporting

Users can report:

- Recipes  
- Images  
- Profiles  
- Comments  

for violating community standards.

When a report is submitted:

1. It enters our moderation queue  
2. A moderator reviews it  
3. Action is taken if necessary  
4. The reporting user may receive a confirmation message

---

## 5. Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and frequency of violations, the following may occur:

- **Content removal**  
- **Temporary posting restrictions**  
- **Temporary account suspension**  
- **Permanent account ban**  
- **Deletion of all content**  
- **Limitation of features** (such as commenting or posting)

Severe violations (e.g., illegal content, hate speech) may result in immediate permanent suspension.

---

## 6. Appeal Process

If you believe your content was removed or your account was restricted in error, you may file an appeal.

To appeal:

- Contact us at **dev@gobbleguideapp.com**
- Include:
  - Your account email  
  - The content in question  
  - Reason for appeal  

A moderator will review your appeal and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

---

## 7. Transparency

We aim to be transparent by:

- Displaying warnings if content violates guidelines  
- Informing users when their content is removed  
- Providing clear reasons for moderations actions wherever possible  

---

## 8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated occasionally.

- Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date  
- Continued use of Gobble Guide means acceptance of any updated policies  

---

## 9. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Content Moderation Policy, you can contact us at:  
**dev@gobbleguideapp.com**
