# Gobble Guide – Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** 14/11/2025

Welcome to **Gobble Guide**!  
Our community is built on creativity, kindness, and a shared love for food. These Community Guidelines help make Gobble Guide a safe, inspiring, and enjoyable place for everyone.

By using Gobble Guide, you agree to follow these guidelines in addition to our **Terms of Use**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Content Moderation Policy**.

---

## 1. Our Community Values

We encourage users to:

- Share authentic, original recipes  
- Inspire others with creative food ideas  
- Engage respectfully and helpfully  
- Celebrate diverse cultures, cuisines, and cooking styles  
- Contribute to a welcoming, supportive environment  

Gobble Guide thrives when everyone participates with positivity and respect.

---

## 2. General Rules

To keep our community safe and supportive, the following rules apply to all users.

### ✔️ You **may**:

- Post original recipes, food images, and cooking tips  
- Save, organize, and engage with recipes from other users  
- Comment respectfully and constructively (once the feature is available) 

### ✖️ You **may not**:

- Post harmful, hateful, explicit, or illegal content  
- Upload images or text you don’t own or have rights to  
- Mislead others about ingredients, nutrition, or food safety  
- Spam or promote products/services without permission  
- Bully, harass, or insult other users  
- Post violent, graphic, or disturbing materials  
- Share private or personal information (your own or others’)  

---

## 3. Posting Content Responsibly

### 3.1 Be Original  
Only upload content that you created or have the right to use. Respect the work of other creators, authors, and photographers.

### 3.2 Keep It Food-Focused  
This is a cooking and recipe community. Non-food or irrelevant content may be removed.

### 3.3 Share Safe Practices  
Avoid posting recipes or instructions that may cause harm, including:

- Unsafe food handling  
- Undercooked foods without proper warnings  
- Unsupported health claims  
- Advice that replaces professional medical guidance  

If you share content with potential risks, please include a note or warning.

---

## 4. Respectful Interaction

### ✔️ Positive Interactions  
We encourage:

- Supportive and constructive comments  
- Sharing tips, alternatives, and helpful suggestions  
- Respect for cultural food traditions and dietary choices  

### ✖️ Unacceptable Behavior  
Not allowed:

- Insults, harassment, or targeted attacks  
- Discriminatory language  
- Mocking or dismissing others’ food choices  
- Trolling, spamming, or provoking arguments  

Treat others how you'd like to be treated. Food brings people together—let’s keep it that way.

---

## 5. Protecting the Community

We take community safety seriously. The following are **not permitted**:

### 5.1 Harmful or Dangerous Content  
Content that encourages unsafe behavior, injuries, or illegal acts will be removed.

### 5.2 Adult or Sexual Content  
Gobble Guide is a family-friendly platform. Nudity, sexualization, or adult content is not allowed.

### 5.3 Violence or Graphic Imagery  
Violent or graphic images—even if food-related—are prohibited.

### 5.4 Misinformation  
Do not share misleading or false claims about:

- Nutrition  
- Allergies  
- Medical benefits  
- Health outcomes  

Recipes are meant to help and inspire, not endanger.

---

## 6. Advertising, Promotion & Spam

We want Gobble Guide to remain authentic and useful.

Not allowed:

- Self-promotion or advertising without permission  
- Posting affiliate links  
- Referral links  
- Unsolicited promotions  
- Repetitive or automated posts  
- Bots or scraping tools  

You may share personal social media handles **only in your profile bio**, not in posts or comments.

---

## 7. Reporting & Enforcement

### 7.1 Reporting  
If you see content that violates these guidelines:

- Use the in-app **Report** feature  
- Select the reason that best fits the issue  

Reports help keep the community safe.

### 7.2 What Happens After a Report  
Depending on severity, we may:

- Remove content  
- Issue warnings  
- Temporarily restrict accounts  
- Permanently ban accounts  

Severe violations may result in immediate termination.

---

## 8. Appeals

If you believe a moderation action was a mistake, you may appeal by contacting:  
**dev@gobbleguideapp.com**

Include:

- Your username or email  
- A link or screenshot of the content  
- A brief explanation  

We will review the appeal as soon as possible.

---

## 9. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines to reflect changes in community behavior, platform requirements, or legal standards.  
Updates will be indicated by the “Last Updated” date.

Continued use of the app means you agree to the updated guidelines.

---

## 10. Thank You for Being Part of Gobble Guide

Our mission is to make cooking simple, enjoyable, and inspiring.  
Thank you for helping build a positive, creative, and respectful community.

If you have questions, contact us at:  
**dev@gobbleguideapp.com**
