# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2025

Welcome to **Gobble Guide** (“we,” “our,” or “us”).  
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app, website, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By using Gobble Guide, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

### a. Information You Provide
When you use our Services, you may provide information such as:
- Name and contact details (e.g., email address)
- Account credentials
- Payment or billing details (if applicable)
- Feedback, support requests, or other communications

### b. Automatically Collected Information
When you use Gobble Guide, we may automatically collect:
- Device and browser information
- App usage data (pages viewed, time spent, actions taken)
- Log data and crash reports

### c. Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use cookies and similar tools to:
- Keep you signed in
- Remember preferences
- Analyze app and website performance

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser or device settings.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
- Personalize your user experience
- Respond to support requests
- Communicate updates, security alerts, and policy changes
- Analyze usage and improve features
- Ensure compliance with legal obligations

We will only process your personal data when we have a legal basis to do so, such as your consent, our legitimate interests, or compliance with applicable laws.

---

## 3. How We Share Information

We do **not** sell your personal information.

We may share your data with:
- **Service providers** that help us operate our Services (e.g., hosting, analytics, or payment processors)
- **Legal authorities**, when required by law
- **Business partners**, in case of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (we’ll notify you if this happens)

All third parties are required to protect your data and use it only as directed by us.

### Third-Party Services and SDKs

We may use trusted third-party services (SDKs) to help us operate and improve Gobble Guide.  
These services may collect certain data as part of their functionality.  
Section 11 provides a list of the primary SDKs we use and their purposes.
Each third-party SDK is integrated in accordance with their respective privacy policies and complies with applicable data protection laws.

## 4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary to:
- Provide our Services
- Comply with legal requirements
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our agreements

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access and receive a copy of your personal data
