## 4. Importing Recipes and AI Processing

Gobble Guide lets you import a recipe from a link or from photos and screenshots. How your information is handled depends on the method you choose.

### Importing from links

When you paste or share a link, the App sends that link to our servers, which fetch the public text of the page on your behalf (for example a recipe page, or the title, description or caption of a YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Pinterest post). That text is returned to your device. We do not use your login to those platforms, and we only read publicly available content. The platforms that host the link may log the request under their own privacy policies.

### Importing from photos and screenshots

Text in your photos is read **on your device** using Google ML Kit text recognition. The images themselves are not uploaded for this step. Only the text extracted from them is used for import, and, if you choose Direct import, sent as described below.

### Direct AI import (Google Gemini)

If you tap a **Direct import**, the recipe text extracted from your link or photos, together with your chosen settings (such as units and "rewrite in my own words"), is sent through our servers to **Google Gemini** (Google LLC), which returns a structured recipe that fills in the form.

- We send only the recipe text and your import settings. We do not send your name, email address, or account identifiers to Google. 
- Google processes this text under its own terms. 
- We do not store the text you send for import or Gemini's response beyond what is needed to complete the request, other than the recipe you choose to save.
- We record that a direct import was completed so we can apply the weekly limit for free accounts.

Direct import is optional. You can instead use **"Use my own AI"**: the App creates a prompt that you copy and paste into an AI assistant of your choice, then paste the result back. In that case nothing is sent to Google Gemini by us, and the third-party assistant you choose handles the text under its own privacy policy.

AI-generated results may be inaccurate. Please review imported recipes before relying on them.

### Sharing into the App

If you share a link or text into Gobble Guide from another app, we read only the first link in what you share to start an import. We do not access other content in the source app.
