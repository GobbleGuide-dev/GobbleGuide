# Account Requests

Gobble Guide is committed to protecting your privacy and giving you control over your personal data.  
You can contact us at any time to **request access, updates, or deletion** of your account or personal information.

---

## 1. Types of Requests

You can request the following:

### a. Account Access
- Receive a copy of the personal information we hold about your account.
- Understand what data we collect and how it is used.

### b. Account Update
- Correct inaccurate or outdated information in your account.
- Update your email address, profile details, or preferences.

### c. Account Deletion
- Permanently delete your account and all associated data.
- Note: Some information may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., payment records).

---

## 2. How to Submit a Request

To submit a request, please email us at:

**dev@gobbleguideapp.com**  

Include the following information in your email:

1. **Full Name** (as registered with your account)  
2. **Email Address** associated with your Gobble account  
3. **Type of Request** (Access / Update / Deletion)  
4. **Details** of your request (e.g., what information you want updated or deleted)  

> Example email subject: `Gobble Guide Account Request: Deletion`

---

## 3. Verification

For security reasons, we may need to **verify your identity** before fulfilling your request.  
This ensures that only the account owner can access or modify their data.
